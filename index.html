About ChooseCanuck

Building economic resilience through informed consumer choices.

Our Mission

ChooseCanuck helps Canadians find Canadian-made products with a real brand website — so you can support domestic makers with one click.

How It Works

  • Search — Type any product to find Canadian makers
  • Categories — Browse by category
  • Regions — Discover products from your province or territory
  • Submit — Add products with an official website

Listing rule

Every product in the database is Canadian-origin and includes an official website. No website, no listing.

Why It Matters

Trade disputes, tariffs, and geopolitical shifts can disrupt supply chains. By knowing what's made in Canada and supporting domestic production, we build resilience. Every purchase is a vote for the economy we want.
